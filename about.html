About Crezia

Who we are and what drives us

Crezia is a team-built web project created to practice real-world collaboration using Git and GitHub. Every line of code here was written by a different contributor, reviewed, and merged — just like professional teams do it.

Our goal is simple: learn by doing. We split features into issues, create branches, open pull requests, and review each other's work before anything lands on main.

Whether you are a beginner or someone brushing up on team workflows, Crezia is a safe space to practice, make mistakes, and grow as a developer.
